# rhinoceros_2_swallowed
The Rhinoceros Emperor Game (also called the Rhinoceros Emperor Bureau or 犀皇局) is a legendary, highly demanding comprehension test and puzzle from the Chinese web novel.

Origins and Purpose
Creator: Created by the Rhinoceros Emperor, an ancient and powerful God King from the ancient Wu Nation, as his ultimate legacy.
The Duan Dong River Inheritance: It serves as a brutal trial of comprehension and willpower for candidates trying to claim the prestigious Duan Dong River heritage. 
Nature of the Test: Studying the game feels like a continuous soul attack. It drains and tests a cultivator's willpower and mental fortitude.
Rules and Mechanics
Complexity: It features thousands of sequential spatial and divine power puzzles, requiring deep insight into foundational units and micro-structures.
High Stakes: In tests like the inheritance trials, candidates (such as Luo Feng) must solve a massive quota of sequential puzzles within strict time limits or face lethal consequences.
Personal Insight: Because solutions involve profound structural variations of divine power and logic, copying other beings is impossible; it truly measures individual comprehension.

While there isn't an official 3D spatial software or application released by the creators of Swallowed Star to replicate the exact "10,080 phantom arrays" on a PC, you and your friend can easily bring the Rhinoceros Emperor Game to life right now! 
Since the lore dictates that the game requires discovering hidden spatial connections to form a "perfectly sealed energy matrix loop", the closest real-world equivalents are logical loop-closing games, spatial strategy matrixes, or a custom-made interactive version.
Here is a simplified, playable implementation of the basic mechanics inspired by the first few levels of the trial, where you must form complete loops using specific elements, followed by ways you can challenge your friend!
